Delete your account

Required account-deletion information for tQlendar (Google Play / App Store)

You can request permanent deletion of your tQlendar account and associated personal data. This page explains what is deleted, what may be retained briefly, and how to submit a request.

What gets deleted

  • Your account profile and authentication linkage used by tQlendar
  • Calendars you own and their daily entries
  • Collaborator invitations tied to your owned calendars
  • App data associated with your user ID in our database

What may remain temporarily

  • Limited backup or security logs for a short period, then removed or anonymized
  • Information we must keep if required by law
  • Content inside calendars owned by someone else where you were only a collaborator (the owner controls that data; your membership is removed)

How to request deletion

  1. Email support@truelinq.com from the same email you use to sign in.
  2. Use the subject line: tQlendar account deletion
  3. Include your sign-in email (Google or Apple) and confirm you want permanent deletion.
  4. We will verify the request and delete your account data within 30 days (usually sooner).
Request deletion by email Read Privacy Policy

After deletion

You will no longer be able to sign in to tQlendar with that account. If you install the app again later, you will need to create a new account and start fresh.
