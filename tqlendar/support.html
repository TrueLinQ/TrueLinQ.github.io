Common questions

  • How do I create a tracker? Sign in, tap New tracker, pick a template or Custom, then add fields. See Create a tracker.
  • How do I share with family? Open a tracker you own → More → Share, then invite by email or one-time link. See Share & invitations.
  • Can I customize fields? Yes — options, numbers, free text, or long text. Browse starters on Templates.
  • How do I delete my account? Follow the steps on our Delete Account page.
