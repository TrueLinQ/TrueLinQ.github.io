Common questions

  • How do I create a calendar? Sign in, then tap New calendar and add the fields you want to track.
  • How do I share with family? Open a calendar you own and invite collaborators by email.
  • Can I customize fields? Yes — use options, numbers, or notes for routines like milk delivery or maid attendance.
